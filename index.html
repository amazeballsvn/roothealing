Root Healing Uploader

A publishing tool for the Root Healing channel

Root Healing makes short educational videos about health and the human body, in English on YouTube and in Vietnamese on TikTok. The channel has no sponsors and does not promote any brand or drug company.

What the uploader does

  • It runs on the channel owner's own computer and is used only by the channel owner.
  • It signs in to the Root Healing TikTok account with TikTok's official Login Kit.
  • It uploads videos that Root Healing made itself, with their captions and hashtags, through TikTok's official Content Posting API, either as drafts to review in the TikTok app or as posts.
  • It does not read, collect or post anything for other TikTok users.
