Privacy Policy

Root Healing Uploader · Effective 7 October 2026

Root Healing Uploader ("the app") is a tool that uploads Root Healing's own videos to the Root Healing TikTok account. It is operated by the owner of the Root Healing channel ("we") and is used only by the channel owner. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Who uses the app

The app is not offered to the public. The only TikTok account that signs in to it is the Root Healing account, owned by us.

Data the app receives from TikTok

When we sign in with TikTok's Login Kit, TikTok gives the app:

  • an access token and a refresh token, so the app can upload on behalf of the Root Healing account;
  • basic profile information of that account (its open ID, display name and avatar), used only to confirm that the right account is signed in;
  • the status of each upload (for example, whether a video was processed and published), so the app can report errors.

The app requests only the permissions it needs: user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

Data the app sends to TikTok

The video files we choose to upload, with their captions, hashtags and post settings. All videos are made by Root Healing.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a private file on the channel owner's computer. They are not sent to any server of ours, as we run no servers for the app. The app keeps no database and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. This website uses no cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data goes only between the owner's computer and TikTok, as needed to upload videos.

Other people's data

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other TikTok user, including viewers, followers or commenters.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions), and the token file is then deleted from the owner's computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the channel owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
